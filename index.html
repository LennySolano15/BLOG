CRITICAL THINKING & MEDIA LITERACY

Think Before You Share

The internet gives us access to a lot of information, but not everything online is true. Critical thinking means asking questions before accepting information as a fact. Media literacy helps us identify the source, check the date, compare information and recognize possible bias.

When I read something online in English, I try to understand the main idea, look for evidence and compare it with another reliable source. This improves both my English and my ability to make informed decisions.

English lesson: fact, opinion, source, evidence, bias and misinformation.

⚖️🚔
CRIME & PUNISHMENT

Crime, Punishment and Responsibility

Crime is an illegal activity such as robbery, arson or assault. Punishment is a penalty imposed after a person is convicted of a crime. Some examples include a fine, a traffic ticket, license suspension or house arrest.

This topic helped me learn useful vocabulary such as abduction, armed robbery, arson, assault, attempted murder, burglary and domestic violence. It also helped me understand that criminal law and civil law are different areas of law.

English lesson: crime, law, punishment and responsibility vocabulary.

🌍🤝
CULTURAL LITERACY

Understanding Other Cultures

Cultural literacy helps us understand how people from different societies live, communicate and celebrate their traditions. Learning about other cultures can help us show respect and avoid stereotypes.

For example, greetings, schedules, traditions and social rules can be different from one country to another. Learning these differences in English helps me develop language skills and a wider view of the world.

English lesson: traditions, customs, respect and cultural differences.
